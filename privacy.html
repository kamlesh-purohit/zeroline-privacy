Privacy Policy — Zeroline

Last updated: 9 September 2026

Zeroline is a personal debt and money tracker for Android. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your records stay on your phone. There is no Zeroline account, no server that holds your data, no analytics and no advertising.

1. Who runs this app

Zeroline is published by Kamlesh Purohit. Contact for any question about this policy or your data: kamleshtpurohit@gmail.com.

2. What Zeroline stores, and where

Everything you enter — debts, payments, income, subscriptions, expenses, cards you are holding for other people, trips, daily reviews and your written plan — is stored in a database inside the app's own private storage on your device.

  • There is no account and no sign-in.
  • In the current Android build, financial records are not uploaded by Zeroline. User-initiated exports and earlier cloud-enabled betas are described below.
  • Android's automatic cloud backup is switched off for this app (allowBackup="false"), so your records are not copied to Google Drive or transferred to a new device by the system.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

You can export a backup yourself, at any time, from Settings. That produces a plain JSON file which you save to a location you choose. Where that file then lives, and who can read it, is under your control.

3. What leaves your device

The current Android build uses the network for exchange-rate lookups and SDK diagnostics, as described below.

Reading a screenshot or receipt

Text recognition runs entirely on your phone using a model bundled inside the app. Images you select are never uploaded anywhere. Zeroline uses the Android photo picker, so it has no permission to browse your gallery — it only ever receives the single image you pick.

Google ML Kit, the bundled text-recognition SDK, collects diagnostic and usage metadata such as device/app information, per-installation identifiers, feature events, configuration, timing and error codes. Google documents this collection at ML Kit data disclosure. This metadata is encrypted in transit. This is separate from receipt image contents, which Zeroline does not upload.

On-device guidance and earlier beta builds

Android version code 4 and later disable cloud advisor chat and API-key entry. The Advisor computes guidance locally. Earlier private beta builds offered optional Anthropic chat using a user-provided key; those requests sent financial summaries and chat to Anthropic under the user's Anthropic account terms. Update to the current build to stop using that feature.

Currency rates for trips

If you record a trip expense in a foreign currency, Zeroline asks a public exchange-rate service (frankfurter.dev) what that currency was worth in rupees on a given date.

That request contains only a three-letter currency code and a date. It does not include your amount, merchant, or trip records. Like other Internet services, the recipient can receive connection metadata such as your IP address.

4. What Zeroline does not do

  • No Zeroline-operated analytics. Google ML Kit diagnostics and usage metadata are described above.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No advertising or marketing SDKs. The text-recognition SDK reports technical diagnostics.
  • We do not sell personal information. SDK technical-data collection is described above.
  • No location collection.
  • No access to your contacts, messages, call logs or files.

5. Permissions, and why

  • Internet — for exchange-rate requests and SDK diagnostics described in section 3.
  • Notifications — to remind you before an EMI or subscription is due. Reminders are generated and scheduled on your device.
  • Run at startup — so reminders survive a restart. Alarms do not survive a reboot on their own.
  • Vibrate — for reminder notifications.

6. Children

Zeroline is a personal finance tool intended for adults. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Your control over your data

Because your financial records in the current build remain on your device, you do not need to ask us for access to it or deletion of it — we do not hold it. You can view and edit every record in the app, export it all to a JSON file, and delete everything by uninstalling.
