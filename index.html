Privacy Policy — Zeroline

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Zeroline is a personal debt and money tracker for Android and iPhone. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your records stay on your devices. There is no Zeroline account, no Zeroline server that holds your data, no analytics and no advertising. On iPhone your records can sync through your own iCloud account, and if you choose to add your own AI key, the questions you ask the Guide are sent to that AI provider.

1. Who runs this app

Zeroline is published by Kamlesh Purohit. Contact for any question about this policy or your data: kamleshtpurohit@gmail.com.

2. What Zeroline stores, and where

Everything you enter — debts, payments, income, subscriptions, expenses, imported statement entries, cards you are holding for other people, trips and who is on them, daily reviews and your written plan — is stored in a database inside the app's own private storage on your device.

  • There is no Zeroline account and no sign-in.
  • Zeroline does not upload your records to us. We never receive them.
  • On Android, the system's automatic cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are switched off for this app, so your records are not copied to Google Drive or moved to a new phone by the system. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
  • On iPhone, your records are kept in sync between your own Apple devices using the private database of your iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit). That database is tied to your Apple ID and we cannot read it. If iCloud is off or unavailable, the records stay on the phone only. You can remove the synced copy from iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage storage.

You can export a backup yourself, at any time, from Settings. That produces a plain JSON file which you save to a location you choose. Where that file then lives, and who can read it, is under your control.

3. What leaves your device

Reading screenshots, receipts and statements

Text recognition runs on your device: Google ML Kit's bundled model on Android and Apple's Vision framework on iPhone. Images and PDF statements you choose are read on the device and not uploaded, with one exception you control: on iPhone, if you have added a Gemini key (see below) and switch on Let Gemini read screenshots in Settings, a screenshot you choose to capture is sent to Google's Gemini API to be read. That option is off unless you turn it on, and statement PDFs are always read on the device. If a statement PDF is password-protected, the password is used on the device to open it and is not stored or sent anywhere. Zeroline uses the system photo picker, so it has no permission to browse your gallery — it only receives the image you pick.

Sorting unfamiliar shop names into categories can use an on-device language model where your phone has one: Gemini Nano through Android's AICore, or Apple's on-device Foundation Models on iPhone. These run on the phone; the shop names are not sent to us or to Google or Apple for this.

On Android, Google's ML Kit SDKs collect diagnostic and usage metadata such as device and app information, per-installation identifiers, feature events, configuration, timing and error codes. Google documents this at ML Kit data disclosure. It does not include the contents of your images or records.

The Guide and optional cloud AI (iPhone)

The Guide works on the device by default. On iPhone you may paste your own API key for Anthropic (Claude) or Google (Gemini) in Settings. Only if you do, and only when you ask the Guide a question, the app sends that provider your conversation together with a summary of your finances: income, debt balances, interest rates and EMIs, subscriptions, amounts owed on friends' cards, and the payoff plan. Account numbers, card digits and your notes are never included. That request goes directly from your phone to the provider under your own account with them and their terms; it does not pass through us. Your key is kept in the iPhone Keychain on that device only. With a Gemini key you can also choose to have screenshots read by Gemini, as described above. Remove the key in Settings to stop all of this.

The Android app does not offer cloud AI chat. Some early private beta builds of the Android app did, using a key you supplied; updating to the current build removes it.

Currency rates for trips and foreign spends

If you record an expense in a currency other than your account's own, Zeroline asks a public exchange-rate service (frankfurter.dev) what that currency was worth on a given date. The request contains only the currency codes and a date — not your amount, merchant or trip. Like any Internet service, the recipient can see connection metadata such as your IP address.

4. What Zeroline does not do

  • No Zeroline-operated analytics or tracking. The ML Kit diagnostics above are Google's.
  • No advertising, no advertising identifiers and no advertising SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information. We never receive your records.
  • No location collection.
  • No access to your contacts, messages, call logs, camera or files beyond those you pick yourself.

The app contains code for an optional shared-sync feature that is switched off in current builds and cannot be turned on. If it is ever enabled, this policy will be updated first to describe it.

5. Permissions, and why

  • Internet — for exchange rates, the optional cloud AI on iPhone, iCloud sync on iPhone, and the SDK diagnostics described in section 3.
  • Notifications — to remind you before an EMI or subscription is due. Reminders are generated and scheduled on your device.
  • Run at startup (Android) — so reminders survive a restart.
  • Vibrate (Android) — for reminder notifications.

6. Children

Zeroline is a personal finance tool intended for adults. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Your control over your data

Because your records stay on your devices (and, on iPhone, in your own iCloud), you do not need to ask us for access to them or deletion of them — we do not hold them. You can view and edit every record in the app, export it all to a JSON file, and delete everything by uninstalling the app and, on iPhone, removing Zeroline's data from iCloud storage. For data sent to Anthropic or Google through your own API key, their policies and your account with them apply.
